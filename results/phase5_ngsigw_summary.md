Cells: sigma_P with the Gaussian SIGW (as in phase5_summary.md) -> with the NG SIGW. 'alt': the A^3 truncation for the quadratic template, the A^3 series plus the F^4 A^4 terms for the others. Values above 4 sigma lie beyond the free-spectrum KDE samples.
